### 🚀 About Me
I am a Network Engineer transitioning into the world of offensive and defensive security. With a deep, hands-on foundation in routing, switching, and systems administration, I understand how networks are built—which gives me a unique advantage in understanding how they can be compromised and defended. 

My ultimate goal is to become a **Cyber Investigator**. I am highly motivated to take on complex infrastructure challenges, leveraging my active interest in cloud computing to secure modern, distributed environments.

*   🔭 Currently documenting **13+ practical cybersecurity labs** covering malware analysis, pentesting, and log analysis.
*   🌱 Building advanced skills in **Threat Hunting, SIEM (Splunk/Wazuh), and Vulnerability Assessment**.
*   ⚡ Fun fact: I can configure a router, manage a Linux server, provision cloud resources, and analyze a packet capture—all from the command line!
